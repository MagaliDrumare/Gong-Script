# Demo Script — SensibleAI Forecast Positioning & Architecture

**Source:** Sensible AI Forecast Demo for Regency Centers, 15 May 2026 · Presenter: Matt DeLise, Sales Specialist, AI · Segment: ~6:30–23:30 (~17 min)
**Audience on source call:** Regency Centers (VP Planning, Director Lease Administration, SEC Reporting) + Riveron implementation partner — adapt industry examples (retail REIT / shopping centers) to your prospect.

## At a glance
- **What you demo:** Why SensibleAI Forecast exists within unified planning — where ML adds asymmetric value, the proof metrics (24% accuracy uplift, 86% effort reduction), and the four building blocks: targets, history, features/events, and model arena — ending with explainability as the adoption unlock.
- **Core value message:** Not every forecast needs machine learning — apply it where accuracy and effort reduction **asymmetrically** change decisions, and win adoption with **explainability**, not black-box predictions.
- **Demo environment needed:** Positioning slide deck (`Trusted, secure and unified AI`, `Unified Planning Across Every Methodology`, `SensibleAI Forecast`, `Key Concepts`); no live product until handoff to demo.

## Argumentation structure
1. **Pain** — Finance teams forecast in Excel with hidden dependencies; manual effort limits granularity (monthly only); rule-based methods work for some lines but break down for complex, driver-heavy predictions.
2. **Status quo cost** — Low accuracy on high-impact lines; 86%-level manual effort; forecasts that can't be defended to operators and leadership.
3. **Capability** — SensibleAI Forecast trains 25+ algorithms at every intersection, incorporates internal and external features/events, and produces explainable predictions with scenario modeling.
4. **Proof** — **24% average uplift in accuracy** vs. previous process; **86% reduction in effort**; 150+ customer projects; 12–16 week average kickoff-to-go-live.
5. **Differentiator** — Explainability: finance teams double-click every cell — this solution was built so predictions are defensible, not a "cool science project."
6. **Payoff** — Scoped targets (e.g., base rent, operating portfolio) feed driver-based P&L in OneStream with full lineage; customer can embed capability in current design phase [adapt: Regency targeting 2026 for three-year history].

| # | On screen (what to show/click) | Talk track (what to say) |

| 1 | **Trusted, secure and unified AI** / portfolio slide (Forecast highlighted) | "We're in the Forecast lane today — ML-powered predictions inside unified planning, not a bolt-on data science tool." |
| 2 | **Unified Planning Across Every Methodology** | "OneStream supports a myriad of methodologies — rule-based, driver-based, table-based — and you'll see many of these at Splash with the [adapt: implementation] team. **Not every forecast needs machine learning.** Capital expenses, depreciation — once you've decided your assets, rule-based works very well and gets it right." |
| 3 | Value metrics slide (accuracy + effort) | "The average uplift in accuracy that we've seen is **24 percent** — compared to the previous process. The reduction in effort is about **86 percent**. That typically means the system doing more of the work — and we get to go deeper: sometimes **monthly to weekly or daily**, sometimes **additional dimensions** we couldn't afford to model manually." |
| 4 | Asymmetric impact examples | "Where we focus is **asymmetric impact** — a particular area of the forecast where if we get it more right, or reduce the effort, it changes decision-making or becomes an input to other components. For retail-oriented customers we might predict **sales by location**, then use that as an input to derive the rest of the P&L in a driver-based model — full automation, full lineage, best bang for your buck." |
| 5 | History + internal/external data | "We take into account not just a year of history but **multiple years — typically a minimum of three**. And we're not looking at trends in isolation — we incorporate **internal and external datasets** that inform accuracy. We do this granularly — we'll walk through that in the demo." |
| 6 | **Explainability** — "third leg of the stool" | "Accurate output plus efficiency would be awesome on their own — but if those are the only two things, it becomes a **cool science project**, not something that runs the business. The third leg is **explainability**. If I hand you a forecast broken down across properties, the first thing finance does is open Excel and double-click the cell — what's the calculation? What dependencies? We built this so you can **easily explain the output and make it defensible** to operators and leadership." |
| 7 | **Key Concepts** — Targets | "Four concepts build up an ML forecasting process. First: **targets** — the prediction we want to make. Revenue, expenses, volumes, drivers. For insurance clients that's average cost per claim, incident rates. Once we define the output, we define **dimensions** most important to us." |
| 8 | Dimensions / intersection example (tenant × lease × location) | "[Adapt: Regency example] We might look across tenant categories, lease types, and locations — that combination drives how many **targets** we train. That determines history granularity, environment sizing, and how we scope before we ever start a project. It's probably not 100% dense — certain tenant categories don't get all lease types." |
| 9 | Historical data load | "Historical data could come from anywhere — loaded once. It's not always in OneStream before you start; often one or two systems. We might go to a different system to go back further, then load from the current system going forward." |
| 10 | **Features and events** | "These are potential drivers of the forecasted value — the questions you discuss in forecast meetings: what's happening in this market, how will it affect these tenant categories? We test them **data-driven and objective**. Out of the box we bring GDP, weather data, housing starts — that lets us **accelerate projects to 12–16 weeks** kickoff to go-live. Then we focus project time on datasets specific to [adapt: customer] — subscriptions, internal data, FRED, etc." |
| 11 | Rapid project experimentation | "Bulk of project time is **iterating and testing** combinations of features and events — which combination gives greatest accuracy. In a final model, only a **handful really move the needle** — this lets us prove that." |
| 12 | **Model arena** — 25 algorithms × intersections | "All components go into the **model arena** — 25+ algorithms across **every intersection** we're forecasting. In an example of 6,000 targets × 25 algorithms — **150,000 models** iterated. Training at the lowest level lets the system assign **best fit automatically** via historical backtest, and gives insights to **defend every individual prediction**. Seasonality differences across regions — same lease type, different tenant category — that's what drives accuracy and explainability." |
| 13 | Scenario modeling | "We get **objective scenario modeling** across any loaded variable — interest rates increase at a point in time, solution creates a scenario and drives impact across the rest of the P&L. We also capture relationships **over time** — population data, housing starts — effects that hit a week, two weeks, three months later. Hard to do unless you have something calculating those nuances." |
| 14 | Pause for questions before live demo | "Any initial questions from the team on any of these concepts, the process, or ideas that are coming to mind?" |

## Key messages to land
- **24%** average accuracy uplift vs. previous process (150+ projects).
- **86%** reduction in effort.
- Not every line item needs ML — use it for **asymmetric impact**.
- Minimum **three years** of history typically required.
- **12–16 weeks** average kickoff to go-live (OOB features/events accelerate).
- **25 algorithms** per intersection; best-fit auto-selected via backtest.
- **Explainability** is the adoption unlock — defensible predictions, not a science project.
- Visualizations, processes, and calculations are **already built** — no coding new algorithms per location.
