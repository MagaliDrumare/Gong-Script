# Demo Script — SensibleAI Agents & MCP Positioning

**Source:** Bosselman | OneStream AI Agents, 28 Jul 2026 · Presenter: Wes Johnson, Sales Specialist, AI (+ Logan Shallenberger intro) · Segment: ~8:39–17:24 (~8 min)
**Audience on source call:** Bosselman Administrative Services (CFO, Controller, Assistant Controller) + Holland Parker partner — adapt reconciliation, store-level reporting, and upgrade-path references to your prospect.

## At a glance
- **What you demo:** The architecture story for SensibleAI Agents — governed agentic AI inside OneStream, MCP as the extension layer to external LLMs, and Control Tower for visibility — framed against Bosselman's reconciliation automation and multi-location reporting needs.
- **Core value message:** Maintain **governed data** while leveraging AI to maximize ROI on the OneStream investment — not shadow AI in Copilot disconnected from the system of record.
- **Demo environment needed:** Slide deck (`Governed agentic AI, built for finance.`, `The intelligence layer between OneStream and any AI.`, `Extending OneStream's Finance Agentic Layer with MCP.`, `Control Tower: See everything. Control everything.`); no live product until handoff.

## Argumentation structure
1. **Pain** — Controller team drowning in manual reconciliations and location-level analysis; CFO pushing AI exploration but finance data lives in governed OneStream cubes, not Copilot.
2. **Status quo cost** — Copilot and generic AI aren't integrated with the accounting system; 21 new locations increasing reporting load; upgrades required before agents can run.
3. **Capability** — GA agents + MCP connections: native Finance Analyst inside OneStream, or extend governed context to external AI clients via MCP.
4. **Proof** — Slide narrative on governed vs. ungoverned AI; configuration-based agent understanding (no training on customer financials); Control Tower visibility.
5. **Differentiator** — Agents understand the application through **configuration mechanisms**, not by training on sensitive financial data; separate from ungoverned copy-paste workflows.
6. **Payoff** — Reporting and analysis work becomes more effective while data stays in OneStream; path to automate month-end shareholder emails and store-level drill for accountants.
