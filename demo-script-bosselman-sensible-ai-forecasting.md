## At a glance
- **What you demo:** Sensible AI Forecasting — intersection-based ML models (account × store targets), variable contribution charts for forecast defense, and the path from Excel sheets to governed OneStream forecasting with **25% average accuracy improvement** and **86% effort reduction**.
- **Core value message:** Rich wants the **best forecast with the least effort** — AI learns from historical data at each account-store intersection, explains *why* numbers moved, and doesn't require AI for straightforward calculations (fixed asset sub-ledger pulls).
- **Demo environment needed:** Sensible AI Forecast demo workspace; sample model list showing intersection targets (e.g., region × product or account × store); variable contribution waterfall/chart; volume-based daily forecast example (monthly consumption view).

## Argumentation structure
1. **Pain** — CFO forecasts in Excel: remainder of year vs. budget, then 1–3 years out; multi-day process layered on top of month-end board prep; comp-store plus new stores plus admin rollup.
2. **Status quo cost** — Detail-line forecasting ideal but effort-prohibitive; assumptions entered manually; no systematic variable explanation when board asks "why did this number change?"
3. **Capability** — ML builds one model per intersection (account × store); 2,500 candidate models parsed to 100 active targets; AI infuses time, inflation, and custom variables; straightforward calcs stay deterministic.
4. **Proof** — Live forecast workspace; variable contribution chart (month, quarter, inflation); **25% improvement in accuracy** and **86% reduction in effort** (customer average).
5. **Differentiator** — Per-intersection modeling vs. one-size-fits-all Excel; defendable numbers via variable chart; can follow similar processes to today with systematization and automation.
6. **Payoff** — Forecast lives in OneStream alongside actuals; Rich gets better accuracy with less effort; supports [adapt: 21 new locations] scale without linear Excel pain.
